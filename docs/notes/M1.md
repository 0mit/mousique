# M1 — model, rendering, playback with cursor

## What was done, and seen working

- `packages/core`: the score model (koron, sori, explicit key signatures, a tuning block, unmetered measures), validation, durations, tuning, the timeline, the MEI writer, playback notes (ties merged, grace notes ranked, riz), the metronome beat grid, and the sync-map maths the next milestone needs (both directions, extrapolation, one and zero anchors, local tempo, tempo curve, manual anchors outrank automatic ones). 27 tests, including the Verovio cross-checks.
- `apps/web`: renders the demo phrase with Verovio, plays it with the Web Audio synth from a lookahead scheduler, highlights the sounding note and glides a playhead between onsets, follows the score across systems, and seeks when a note is clicked. Tempo, practice speed (0.25x to 1.25x), metronome, count-in, zoom and the tuning block (A4, global offset, koron and sori sizes) are live. A score can be opened from and saved to JSON, and validation problems are listed.
- A 30-bar score opened from JSON wrapped onto six systems; after clicking a note in bar 21 and playing, the highlighted note and the playhead were on the third system, 8 px apart, and the view had scrolled to it.
- Driven in headless Chromium: the cursor was on the right note mid-phrase (the tied B at bar 2), the recorded pitches were the ones listed in `M0.md`, clicking the C sori moved the position to bar 5, beat 1.5, and playback stopped itself at the end.

## Expected to work, not yet exercised

- Several pages: Verovio is given a page tall enough that a score is one page; very long scores will make several, which the view stacks but nobody has scrolled through yet.
- Touch devices.

## Learned

- Measuring the layout must wait until nothing above the score can still move: the first version measured while a loading message pushed the page down, and the playhead ran 50 px low.
